1Overview

QR Studio is a QR code and barcode scanner and generator app. The App lets you scan QR codes and barcodes using your device camera, generate custom QR codes with optional flyer-style designs, and view a history of your scans and creations. This policy applies to all users of QR Studio on Android.

2Information we collect

We collect the minimum information necessary for the App to function:

Data type Why we access it Leaves your device?
Camera To scan QR codes and barcodes in real time No
Photos / media To let you pick an image as a custom QR background, or scan a QR code from a saved photo No
Scan & generation history To show your past scanned and generated QR codes inside the App No — stored locally on your device only
Advertising ID Used by Google AdMob to show and measure ads Yes — shared with Google AdMob
We do not run our own backend servers that store, log, or transmit your scanned or generated QR code content. Everything you scan or create stays on your device unless you explicitly choose to share it using the App's built-in share feature.

3App permissions explained

  • Camera — required to scan QR codes and barcodes. Camera data is processed on-device only and is never recorded, stored, or uploaded.
  • Photos / media access — used only when you choose to pick a background image for a custom QR design, or when you choose to scan a QR code from an existing photo. We do not browse, access, or upload your photo library beyond the image you explicitly select.
  • Internet access — required to load advertisements via Google AdMob. The App's core scanning and generation features work without an internet connection.

4Information we do NOT collect

  • No account creation, login, username, or password is required to use QR Studio.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other personal identifiers directly.
  • We do not collect precise or approximate location data.
  • We do not read, store, or transmit the content of QR codes you scan or generate to any server we control.
  • We do not sell your data to anyone, under any circumstances.

5Third-party services (Google AdMob)

QR Studio displays advertisements using Google AdMob, a service provided by Google LLC. AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID), approximate region-level information, and app interaction data in order to serve and measure advertisements.

This processing is governed by Google's own privacy policy and advertising policies, not by us directly. We encourage you to review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use AdMob

You can manage ad personalization and reset your Advertising ID at any time through your device's system settings (typically under Settings → Google → Ads on Android).

6Where your data is stored

Your scan and generation history, saved QR designs, and any custom background images you choose are stored locally on your device only, using standard Android app storage. This data is not synced to any cloud service operated by us, and it is automatically removed if you uninstall the App or clear its storage.

7Data sharing & sale

We do not share your personal data with third parties for their own marketing purposes, and we do not sell your data. The only data shared with a third party is the Advertising ID shared with Google AdMob, as described above, solely for the purpose of displaying and measuring in-app advertisements.

8Your choices & controls

  • You can delete individual entries or clear your entire scan/generation history at any time from within the App's History screen.
  • You can revoke Camera or Photos permissions at any time from your device's Settings app; core scanning or image-picker features will simply be unavailable until re-granted.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising via your device's Google Ads settings.
  • Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data associated with it.

9Children's privacy

QR Studio is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the details below and we will take appropriate steps to address it.

10Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect the limited data processed by the App. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal, operational, or regulatory reasons. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
